Her Story: Aisha, Age 10

Aisha was just 10 when she lost her leg in a tragic road accident while walking to rural school. Despite the trauma, Aisha remains one of the brightest and most determined children we’ve met. Her dream is to become a teacher — to help kids like her who can't afford education or mobility support.

Her family struggles financially and cannot afford a prosthetic limb, which costs over RM12,000. With your help, we can change Aisha’s life. Your donation today will fund her prosthetic leg, therapy sessions, and school needs. Let’s give her a chance to stand tall again — and inspire others along the way.
